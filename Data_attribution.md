ANES files were obtained form the American National Election Study website
https://electionstudies.org/data-center/

Gilens_Page.csv is a polling dataset from Gilens and Page (2014) “Testing Theories of American Politics: Elites, Interest Groups, and Average Citizens.” 
It was obtained from their reproduction distribution:
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/perspectives-on-politics/article/abs/testing-theories-of-american-politics-elites-interest-groups-and-average-citizens/62327F513959D0A304D4893B382B992B#supplementary-materials

Federal Election Commission data, including campaign files, donor lists, and lists of donations were downloaded from the FEC website.
https://www.fec.gov/data/browse-data/?tab=bulk-data

Data on voting records of the House on specific bills were manually recorded by myself from the congressional record.
