                 CODE NUMBERS 001 THROUGH 314 ARE SELF REPRESENTING PSU'S.

                 CODE NUMBERS 365 THROUGH 996 ARE NON-SELF REPRESENTING PSU'S


                 EACH PSU IS DENOTED BY A PAIR OF THREE DIGIT CODES (301-302
                 BALTIMORE CITY, FOR EXAMPLE).  THE ALLOCATION OF PAIRS OF
                 CODES TO THE VARIOUS PSU'S HAS TO DO WITH SAMPLING
                 PROCEDURES AND SAMPLING VALIDATION.  THE SAMPLE FROM EACH
                 PSU IS DIVIDED INTO TWO EQUAL GROUPS OF INDIVIDUALS.  ONE
                 GROUP IS GIVEN THE ODD NUMBERED CODE (301 IN OUR EXAMPLE)
                 AND THE OTHER GROUP IS DESIGNATED BY THE PAIR NUMBERED CODE
                 (302).  THE VALIDITY OF THE SAMPLE IS TESTED BY COMPARING
                 ONE GROUP AGAINST THE OTHER.  THIS METHOD IS WIDELY USED IN
                 CONNECTION WITH SELF REPRESENTING PSU'S AND NOT SO WIDELY
                 USED FOR NON-SELF REPRESENTING PSU'S.

>> REF. NO. 19 NOTE, 1966

                 THE FIRST "TYPE OF GOVERNMENT ACTION" RESPONSE REFERS TO
                 THE FIRST PROBLEM AREA MENTIONED BY R IN REF. NO. 19, THE
                 SECOND "TYPE OF GOVERNMENT ACTION" RESPONSE REFERS TO THE
                 SECOND PROBLEM AREA MENTIONED, AND SO ON./

                 THE PURPOSE OF THIS CODE IS TO TRY TO CAPTURE SOME OF THE
                 ADDITIONAL INFORMATION WHICH MANY RESPONDENTS GIVE BEYOND
                 MERELY MENTIONING A PROBLEM AREA.  IF THE R IS CRITICAL OF
                 GOVERNMENT PROGRAMS IN CERTAIN AREAS OR SUGGESTS THAT
                 GOVERNMENT ACTIVITY OR REGULATION IS PARTLY RESPONSIBLE FOR
                 THE PROBLEM, THEN THIS CODE SHOULD ENABLE US TO RETAIN THIS
                 INFORMATION.  IN MOST CASES IN WHICH A PROBLEM AREA IS NAMED
                 WITHOUT ADDITIONAL COMMENT IT WOULD FOLLOW FROM THE WORDING
                 OF THE QUESTION THAT THE RESPONDENT WAS CALLING FOR INCREASED
                 GOVERNMENT ACTIVITY IN THE AREA (CODE 1); E.G., IF THE R
                 JUST SAYS "OLD PEOPLE" OR "POVERTY" WE CAN ASSUME THAT HE
                 WANTS MORE GOVERNMENT ACTION IN THESE AREAS.  ON THE OTHER
                 HAND, IF THE R MENTIONS "TIGHT MONEY" ONE CAN ASSUME HE
                 WANTS THE GOVERNMENT TO RELAX ITS TIGHT CONTROL AND WE
                 WOULD USE CODE 5.  IF THE R MENTIONS THE "CREDIBILITY GAP"
                 WE WOULD ASSUME HE IS BEING CRITICAL OF GOVERNMENT CONTROL
                 OF THE NEWS (CODE 91) AND WOULD USE CODE 3.  IF THE R MEN-
                 TIONS "CORRUPTION IN HIGH PLACES" WE WOULD ASSUME HE WANTS
                 MORE GOVERNMENT REGULATION (CODE 1) TO ASSURE HONESTY IN
                 GOVERNMENT (CODE 80).  IF THE R JUST MENTIONS "VIETNAM" WE
                 WOULD NOT HAVE ANY INDICATION OF WHAT KIND OF ACTION HE
                 THINKS DESIRABLE AND WE WOULD USE CODE 9 (NA).

                 THE DUNCAN SOCIO-ECONOMIC SCALE (1961) IS DERIVED
                 FROM AVERAGE INCOME AND EDUCATIONAL LEVEL OF PERSONS IN EACH
                 CENSUS OCCUPATIONAL CATEGORY.  THE SCORES RANGE FROM 0
                 (TOBACCO MANUFACTURERS) TO 96 (DENTISTS).  DUNCAN SCORES,
                 HOWEVER, ARE NOT AVAILABLE FOR MEMBERS OF THE ARMED FORCES,
                 LIVE-IN LAUNDRESSES AND PERSONS NOT IN THE LABOR FORCE.
                 THESE CATEGORIES ARE CODED 99 IN THE PRECEDING TABLE.
                 CERTAIN ANOMALIES RESULT DUE TO THE FACT THAT THE SCALE
                 SOLELY CONSIDERS AVERAGE INCOME AND EDUCATIONAL LEVEL.  FOR
                 EXAMPLE, CLERGYMEN NORMALLY EARN LOW SALARIES; THEREFORE,
                 THEIR DUNCAN SCORE IS GREATLY BELOW THAN THAT FOUND BY OTHER
                 MORE SUBJECTIVE METHODS.  IN THE BLUE COLLAR SECTION, GREAT
                 DIFFERENCES CAN BE NOTICED AMONG SEMI-SKILLED WORKERS IN
                 VARIOUS INDUSTRIES.  ALSO, ONE FINDS THAT APPRENTICES IN
                 CERTAIN OCCUPATIONS HAVE HIGHER STATUS RATINGS THAN THOSE
                 INDIVIDUALS PERMANENTLY EMPLOYED IN THE SAME OCCUPATION.
                 FOR THESE REASONS, SOME CAUTION HAS TO BE EXERCISED IN
                 MAKING USE OF THE SCALE.  FOR A DETAILED DISCUSSION OF THE
                 SCALE, ITS PROBLEMS AND LIMITATIONS, CONSULT REISS, A.,
                 DUNCAN, O., HATT, P. AND NORTH, C., OCCUPATIONS AND SOCIAL
                 STATUS, NEW YORK:  THE FREE PRESS OF GLENCOE, 1961.

                 MORE THAN ONE UNION IS GIVEN FOR THE PRIMARY UNION MEMBER,
                 THAT UNION MOST CLOSELY ASSOCIATED WITH CURRENT OCCUPATION
                 IS CODED, IF KNOWN.  OTHERWISE, FIRST UNION MENTIONED IS
                 CODED.
